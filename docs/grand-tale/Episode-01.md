# Episode 01 - Aleph's Dream

**Episode title:** Aleph's Dream
**Reference:** [Last Day of Normalcy (TV Tropes)](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/LastDayOfNormalcy)

## Overview

The horn landed on the planet millennia ago and crashed into the side of a mountain, leaving a huge crater. Its presence explains the large concentration of phenomena in that area throughout the story.

Aleph's dream opens with him waking to the sound of the horn, which stays in his mind after he wakes.

## Scenes

### Morning

- Aleph: "Hey Zev, did you hear that?"
- The scene cuts to Zev, still asleep. He answers with an unintelligible grunt.
- Aleph: "Come on, get up! I don't want us to waste any time today!"
- Aleph greets Mother: "Good morning Mother!"
- Mother: "Good morning, sweetheart. The flowers are dead. I'll uproot them tomorrow."
- Aleph: "Aw. They were so beautiful. I wish there was something I could do."

The comment on the natural cycle of life deepens the impact when it is subverted later.

### Departure

- Sister and Zev come outside and greet Mother.
- Mother: "Where are you going, all packed like that?"
- Sister: "We promised Aleph we'd go camp in the hills today so he could stargaze."
- Zev: "Don't worry, we'll be back in time for dinner to celebrate together."
- Mother: "Alright, be careful out there."
- Aleph: "Will do!"
- The trio head out the gate.

### Hike

- Zev: "So which one are we going to today?"
- Aleph: "Hmm... We've been to that one, and that one, and that one... but we've never been to that one!" He points at an unassuming hill further away.
- Sister: "That one it is, then!"
- The scene cuts to a short walk, then a close-up of Zev leading as the two siblings talk behind him.
- Aleph: "Do you think he knows I like him?"
- Sister: "I don't know. Maybe you should just take your shot and see what happens."
- Aleph: "Aww, but it's difficult. What if I get rejected? It would make things so weird. We live together, after all."

### Afternoon (~18:00)

- A montage of activities on the hill.
- Sister reads on the grass while the two boys play with a ball.
- Zev swirls the ball around with magic, then throws it to Aleph.
- Aleph strikes it into the air, where a familiar summoned by Sister catches it and returns it to Zev.
- Zev: "Nice one, better than the last. Let's see if you can hit this one though..."
- Aleph: "Bring it on!"
- The next hit goes high. The familiar tries to catch it, misses, and watches the ball fall down the hillside. It looks at the boys with an embarrassed expression.
- Sister, still reading: "My familiars can't get that far from me. You'll have to go get it."
- Aleph: "Alright, I'll go."
- As Aleph heads down, Zev approaches Sister.
- Sister: "You should just tell him already. He keeps asking me about it."
- Zev blushes.

### The Cavern

- Aleph: "Guys, I can't seem to find it..."
- Zev looks down just as the ground gives way under Aleph and he falls into a cavern.
- Aleph: "Ah!"
- Zev: "Uh oh."
- Aleph: "Ah, there it is."
- Aleph notices his surroundings: "Woah! So beautiful!"
- At the center of the crater is a large, shining object that seems to beckon him.
- When he touches it, he is enveloped in visions of his dream from that morning.
- When he comes to, he sees a small blue creature sitting on top of the object. It vanishes in a blink. He looks around, finds nothing, and puts it down to his dazed state.
- Aleph: "What a weird day."
- He hears the others calling his name. "I'm here! Come check this out!"
- The other two enter the cavern and react in awe. They approach the artifact.
- Sister: "What is that?"
- Aleph: "I don't know. I just found it here."
- Sister: "Should we take it home? Or what do we do with it?"
- Zev: "Maybe we should leave it here."
- Aleph: "I don't know... I have this feeling that it... I can't explain it. It's like it wants me to take it."
- Zev: "Are you sure about this?"
- Aleph: "Come on. Worst case, we can just put it back, right?"
- Zev: "I guess..."
- Sister: "Alright, let's head back up. It's getting dark already."

### Campfire (~20:00)

- The group lies by a small campfire with the artifact on the grass nearby, looking at the starry sky.
- Aleph: "What do you think is out there?"
- Sister and Zev respond with lines that were not written.
- Sister: "Okay, let's head home."

### Birthday (~22:00)

- Aleph places the heavy object on the table. Zev looks at him strangely.
- Zev: "Are you not tired after carrying that all this way?"
- Aleph: "Huh. Coming to think of it, not really. If anything, I feel quite refreshed!"
- Mother: "Ah, just in time for dinner. How was your adventure?"
- Mother: "I'm sorry it couldn't be more _. Things have been rough lately."
- Birthday cake: Bramberry Pie. Make a wish, no candles.
- Aleph: "What if I blew that thing instead? It's an instrument, right? Let's see the sound it makes. It'll be fun!"
- When he blows the horn, Aleph's energy fades and he passes out.
- Sister: "This thing might be dangerous. Let's leave it be for now. Tomorrow we can take it to _ for him to take a look."
- Zev: "I haven't given you your birthday present yet."
- Zev gives a pin or brooch (a little sun, "my little light," possibly ox-themed). Aleph, puzzled, tenses up as Zev kisses him on the cheek.
- Aleph's face turns as red as his hair. Zev slips into his bed.
- Aleph: "Wait, does that mean that..."
- Zev: "Good night!"
- Aleph, to himself: "Ughh, how am I going to sleep now??"
- The scene cuts to the next morning, with Aleph soundly asleep.

### Next Day

- Aleph sits up and looks suspiciously at the still-sleeping Zev.
- They wake and get ready. The dead flowers have revived and grown to twice their previous size.
- Aleph: "Uuh... didn't you say the flowers had all died?"
- Mother: "They... did."
- The artifact shines and shrinks itself into a pendant.
- Sister: "Is this thing listening to us?"
- Aleph: "Alright, let's go get Goldy!"

### Pet Care (~10:00)

- Goldy is bored out of his mind at the pet care, with a bandaged paw and a cone.
- Birdy is in a spa-like state, with cucumbers on her eyes and lotion on her feathers.
- Aleph signs in as a Lightbringer. He confirms it with a cucumber and flies up to greet them.
- Birdy: "Oh thank goodness you're here. I couldn't bear to spend another minute with this insolent cat!"
- Sister: "Hey Birdy, thanks for keeping him company."
- Goldy: "Grrr, I'm not a cat!"
- Goldy: "I'm sorry I missed your birthday."
- Aleph: "It's okay."
- Goldy points his paw at the pendant. "What's that?"
- Aleph: "We don't really know. I found it in a cave while we were hiking yesterday. We're heading to _ to see if he can tell us anything about it."

### Library (~12:00)

- _ examines the artifact: "I've never seen anything like it. Whatever it is, it is immensely powerful."
- _: "You found this yesterday, you said?"
- Aleph: "Yeah."
- _: "Well, it seems The Light has given you a birthday gift. I advise you make good use of it."
- Sister: "Thank you for the help."
