# Pathways

Pathways let players shape the game's progression systems to fit their preferred playstyle.

Whether they play one game mode or many, or enjoy trading or not, Pathways let players customize their experience without missing out on rewards from content they don't engage with.

The system is inspired by Path of Exile's Atlas and Last Epoch's Factions.

## Pathway Types

- Content Atlas
- Player Relationship Atlas

## Example Nodes

- +100% Luck
- -1 Grand Marketplace slot
- Drops are doubled, but the player can no longer use the Grand Marketplace.

## Design Notes

- Last Epoch's Resonance system could be used to create a kind of group ironman.
- Shifts should be organic, and can be respecced rather than locked in permanently.

## References

- Path of Exile Atlas
- Last Epoch Guilds

Last Epoch's guild rank 1 unlocks buying and selling Tier 1 items in the Marketplace.
