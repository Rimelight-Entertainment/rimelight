# The Ordo

A secret society with few but very powerful members. Its members may have minions, but are not necessarily evil. They are closer to anti-heroes and anti-villains, who agree to disagree, in the style of a Magneto vs. Xavier conflict.

## Objective

The Ordo's goal is to collect all the Elder Artifacts. Their reasoning: while enemies could obtain such immense power, the universe will never be free. Because the artifacts are hard to obtain, the Ordo uses the ones it has to help gather the rest. They will only be destroyed once all are gathered together, and destroying them is not easy.

They think beyond good and evil.

## Relationship to the Kinship

Both groups want the artifacts out of the picture in the end, but they disagree on how:

- The Kinship wants the artifacts protected.
- The Kindred want them destroyed.

One side argues it is a matter of proper use. The other believes the world is better off without them existing at all.

The Kinship may see more to the artifacts than being tools with a purpose. They can be learned from, which is why the Kinship hosts the Convergence.
