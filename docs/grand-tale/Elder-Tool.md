## Elderkin Artifacts

The Elderkin Artifacts are relics created by the Elderkin as a means of _. Each was created by one of the Elderkin and represents an aspect of their power. Shortly after creation they were distributed throughout the universe, and they found their way into the history of many worlds and people.

The artifacts have exceptional capability, but only beings with considerable power can use them to their full potential. They can be destroyed, but doing so is very difficult and dangerous and requires a great deal of energy.

Legend says whoever possesses all the artifacts will have powers that rival the Elderkin themselves.

## The Source

**Current whereabouts:** Destroyed.

The Source is an Elderkin Artifact that can create universal laws and contracts and alter the behaviour of reality itself. It is considered the most powerful of the artifacts and has historically been sought after and protected.

**Appearance:** a golden orb, with the frames of its exterior revolving around a core of pure energy.

**Powers and abilities:** its core ability is establishing new behaviours and rules that define how reality works. Its full potential has never been understood.

- Ragnis _ used it during the Fate of the Gods campaign to create a contract stopping the gods from acting against each other, under penalty of losing their power. This ended the Source War.
- Ragnis _ also showed that the Source can indirectly control and bind other beings to one's will. He used this to gather followers to protect him while he siphoned energy from the artifact.

## The Tools

- **String:** creation and shaping of life.
- **Thread:** travel through time, looking through time, stopping time.
- **Memory:** knowledge of the other tools and their recent activity, and the history of objects and people. Could it search for things like a compass?
- **Path:** travel through space, teleportation, creation of portals and gateways.
- **Shell:** protection from harm, including the effects of the other tools. Currently with the Kinship.
- **Kernel:** control over things, manual commanding of movement and actions.
- **Tool:** shaping of land.
- **Alias:** shapeshifting, turning things into other things, transfiguration, emulation. Currently with the Kindred.
