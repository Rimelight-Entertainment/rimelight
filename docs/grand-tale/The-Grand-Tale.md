# The Grand Tale

Draft notes on the in-world organization, not finalized.

## Overview

The Grand Tale is considered a sovereign state. It establishes a kingdom, builds military strength, forms alliances, provides humanitarian aid, completes quests, and generates funds.

Open questions:

- What would it take for someone to be banished from the Grand Tale?
- A character who wants change, but finds it isn't possible this way.
- Quotes: "When you're going to change the world, don't ask for permission." and "I want Hextech to be a tool for us to build a new world."

The Grand Tale is also the developer of the in-universe card game. Who else would know about everything depicted in the cards?

## Kinship Divisions

- **Management:** the division the player actually plays as. The siblings are in charge of it.
- **Labour:** operates the kingdom and estate. Covers cleaning, maintenance, food, construction, and similar work.
- **Crafting**
- **Quest / Operations:** resolves quests around the world, and operations that need specialized personnel, much like Night Raid. Staffed by the strongest Kinship members, apart from the leaders.
- **Military:** handles large-scale conflicts.
- **Intelligence:** writes the Compendium. Tracks living creatures, locations, historic events, artifacts, and more.
