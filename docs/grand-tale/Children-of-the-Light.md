# Children of the Light

Working notes for a story concept. Items are open questions and ideas, not settled canon.

## Premise and Tone

- Grand Tale: Children of the Light items grant a wish when first obtained.
- The horn is like the One Ring, but instead of corrupting its bearer, it has a positive influence.
- Vibe: similar to Avatar: The Last Airbender or Naruto Part 1. Lots of character development and worldbuilding, with the group visiting different cities and factions.
- Open question: should the cast be younger? (Naruto Part 1 with a stronger mentor.)
- Open question: the main cast is around 18 and already part of the GT, but inexperienced. Or is the entire story about the founding of the GT? If so, the current cast is the first iteration of the council.

## Story Structure

- Needs a looming objective, like Sozin's Comet, and a "pain invasion" style arc.
- A seer character should take part in the series.
- Each character gets a 1v1 deathmatch near the end, like Sasuke's retrieval.
- At the end, the group takes over leadership of the GT and moves it into the expansion phase, which becomes the game plotlines.
- Duels between old masters, like Gandalf vs. Saruman, Yoda vs. Palpatine, or the old Hunter x Hunter characters.
- Mentor and training: a wise uncle figure, like Iroh or Dumbledore, who dies in Children of the Light.
- One of the villains becomes an ally later, like Zuko or Gaara.

## Characters and Factions

- The sister has Kazekage-rescue-style vibes.
- Kuvira-type character.
- Tai Lung-type character (Kung Fu Panda).
- Shen-like villain (Kung Fu Panda).
- A masked court, a Black Rose / Court of Owls type group, or a Seven Deadly Sins theme (sprites).
- Some kind of temple.

## Plague Arc

- The group fights a plague and sees suffering, denial, and death.
- Open question: what is the cause? A pathogen, or some wrongdoing, such as a spirit going mad?

## Invoker Arc

- The group meets an ascended founder of the GT.

## Side Arcs and Encounters

- The Eternal Flame Dragon respects Alef as the holder of the Cradle and considers it sacred. He lets them go, saying "Lightbearer," and later returns to strike a deal that lets him live in the map.
- At some point the Eternal Flame Dragon attacks Zev. He warns that if Alef is ever harmed, Zev will face an inferno never seen before. "There will be no creek, no planet, no spell which will save you from inferno."
- The group finds the Cradle in a tree.
- The Grand Wish: uniting all six grants a wish and lets you talk to Glooey.
- A character wants to rid the universe of the artifacts. He manages to summon Glooey and wishes to destroy the artifacts, except the Cradle. Glooey answers, "That one is in good hands." He still chooses not to work with the GT, but is on friendlier terms.
