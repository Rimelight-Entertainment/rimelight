# Guilds

The Guilds system lets players create or join Guilds and take part in special Guild content.

## Guild Contribution

- Guild vs. Guild PvE content, such as contesting a region by scoring against PvE.

## Identity

- Guild name
- Guild crest

## Ownership

If the leader is inactive for 90 days, the deputy owner can claim the guild. As more time passes, the claim moves down the hierarchy.

Guild levels run from 1 to 15. Maximum members per branch rises to 250 at level 15.
