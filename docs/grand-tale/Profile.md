# Profile

## Tabs

- Summary
- History
- Statistics
  - Collection logs are listed separately inside this interface.
  - Boss encounters, split into Solo and Group.
- Achievements

## Achievements

Object colour: light blue.

- **Overview:** total achievement completion, as an absolute number and a percentage, for the total and for each category.
- **Suggested Achievement:** the achievement closest to completion.
- **Recent Progress:** the achievement with the most recent progress.
- **Recent Achievements:** the 5 most recently completed achievements.
- **Filters:** All, Completed, Not completed, With Reward.

## Achievement Rewards

Rewards listed: Taler Level, Mount, Equipment, Saddle, Pet Equipment, Collar, Ribbon.
