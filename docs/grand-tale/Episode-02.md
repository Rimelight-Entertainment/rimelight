## Story Notes

- Zev is illiterate when they first meet. Over time he learns from his sister, but still has some challenges by the time of Children of the Light.
- At its core, Grand Tale is a story about a boy and his light, which can tackle a range of other themes.
- The Grand Tale island is on a creature's back. Aleph befriended it.

## Playing Cards Scene

- Aleph: "What are these? Playing cards?" He grabs a handful and shuffles them.
- Zev: "The illustrations are beautiful!"
- Aleph: "Wait, is that me? Why is my card so weak?"
- Card sales could help fund the Grand Tale. Someone helped with the press at the academy: "If we find a way to scale up production, we could use the gold from card sales to help fund the Grand Tale."
