## Mounts

- After a 1-second channel, players can summon a mount.
- While mounted, movement speed is fixed at 500. It is not affected by the character's stats or buffs.
- Mounts cannot be summoned during combat. If combat starts while mounted, the mount is dismissed immediately.
- The default base mount is a brown horse.
- An icon on the HUD toggles between ground and mounted movement.
- Mounts give no stats beyond the movement speed increase. Cosmetic overrides are purely visual.

## Teleportation Matrix

**Lore:** created by accident by a goblin in Dominion, who was trying to do something else (steal something from somewhere)? (open question)

In one edition of the Postbag From the Hedge, Mr. Mordaut explains teleportation. Runestones allow teleportation by boring a hole, called a teleportation matrix, into an abyss, then looping out of it to arrive somewhere else. There are strict rules: illegal destinations are usually teleblocked, though there are alternate ways around this.

**Functionality:**

- Players open the Teleportation Matrix from the HUD. It shows a menu of teleport options.
- Some options are locked and must be unlocked through quests or other means.
- Players can pin up to 10 destinations to the top of the list.
- Using the matrix requires Teleportation Cores. Cores are made with the Engineering proficiency, or bought from other players.
- Cores in item form can be consumed to add charges to the matrix, so players don't have to carry the item.
- Different destinations cost different amounts of cores, so some are much more expensive than others.
- Teleporting has a 3-second cast time. It can be used in combat, but crowd control can interrupt it.
