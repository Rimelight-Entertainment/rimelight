# Behaviour

## Behaviour Score

Punishments: negative gold from reports, similar to Lost Ark.

| Behaviour Score | Feature                         |
| --------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 3,000           | Unthrottled Communication       |
| 5,000           | Ranked Matchmaking              |
| 6,000           | Access to Text Chat             |
| 7,000           | In-Game Tipping                 |
| 8,000           | Access to Voice Chat            |
| 10,000          | Coaching; Ping Allied Abilities |

Rewards must be careful not to be exploitable.

## Communication Score

By default, all pings, alerts, emotes, chat wheels, and similar actions share a 30-second cooldown.

Players can receive commendations for enemies and allies.

## Abuse Reports

Report types: Chat Abuse, Voice Abuse, Absence, and Disruptive Behaviour.

- Reports can be submitted for players on any team, before, during, or after a game.
- There is no cap on how many reports a player can submit.
- Players are notified when a report succeeds.

## Penalties

### Queue Dodge Penalty Tiers

| Tier | Queue Lock | Rating Change |
| ---- | ---------- | ------------- |
| 1    | 15 minutes | -5            |
| 2    | 30 minutes | -10           |
| 3    | 24 hours   | -15           |

### Absence Penalty Tiers

When players go AFK or leave games, they move up a penalty tier. When they play games without going AFK, the tier slowly decreases back toward 0.

| Tier | Queue Lock |
| ---- | ---------- |
| 1    | 5 minutes  |
| 2    | 30 minutes |
| 3    | 7 days     |

## Matchmaking

Players you have commended are more likely to appear in your future matches, as allies or enemies. Players you have disliked are less likely to appear.
