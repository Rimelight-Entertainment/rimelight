# History

- Shows the 20 most recent games.
- Replays are not saved for games older than that. Players can download a replay while it's available, or save up to 50 in the cloud (probably a paid feature).

## Recent Activity

A summary covering the last 120 days:

- Games played and hours played.
- Good and bad days, based on win/loss ratio.
- Most commonly played modes, with percentages.
- Most commonly played hero in each mode, with percentages.

## Match Details

- Map and match time.
- Total team K/D/A and winning/losing team.
- Structures destroyed.
- Per player:
  - Hero
  - Individual K/D/A and ratio
  - Items and build path/order
  - Talents and order
  - Abilities and order
  - Kill participation
  - CS per minute
  - Heat map
  - Net worth graph
  - Experience graph
  - Damage dealt graph
  - Damage received graph
  - Item and talent stats, such as total time active and total bonus damage
