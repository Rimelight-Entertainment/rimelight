# Journey

## Content Cadence

Grand Tale's new content is released in yearly instalments. Each Year has four Seasons, and each Season lasts three months.

Every Year has a narrative and thematic focus. Each Season within that Year has subthemes that relate to the Year's narrative.

Each Season adds new game content, usually including:

- Additions to the Roster
- New gameplay features, or expansions to existing ones
- New cosmetics

Some Seasons bring temporary events, but most new content is intended to stay permanently after the Season ends.

A new Season also resets all Ranked Ladders and Seasonal Leaderboards.

Some listed content may need to be unlocked or purchased.

## Seasonal Journey

### Attendance

Each day of the week has a buff for some activity, similar to GC Mobile.

| Reward  | Tale Coins | Requirement                        |
| ------- | ---------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Daily   | 25         | Log in at least once that day      |
| Weekly  | 150        | Log in at least 4 days that week   |
| Monthly | 300        | Log in at least 20 days that month |

Total per month: 750 + 600 + 300 = 1,650

### Challenges

Rewards are paid in Tale Coins. Challenges are grouped as Daily, Weekly, and Seasonal.

- Professional Circuit: win using heroes from an actual professional match.
