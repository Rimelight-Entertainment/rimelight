This site doesn't actually use cookies, but this page demonstrates how a cookie notice and policy would be set up.

## 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you load web pages in your browser. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

## 2. How we use cookies

If this site were to use cookies, it would use them to:

- Remember your preferences and settings.
- Enable basic security and authentication features.
- Analyze site traffic to improve user experience.
