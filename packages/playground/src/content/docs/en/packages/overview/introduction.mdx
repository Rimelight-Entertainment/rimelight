## Web Packages

We develop and use a series of web packages internally and distribute them freely as open source packages under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and distributed through [npm](https://www.npmjs.com).

All of our web packages are developed within the context of a [pnpm monorepository](https://pnpm.io/workspaces) at the [rimelight](https://github.com/Rimelight-Entertainment/rimelight) repository.

## Unreal Engine Plugins

We develop and use a series of Unreal Engine plugins internally and distribute them freely as open source packages under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and distributed through [Fab](https://www.fab.com).

Each lives in its own repository under the [Rimelight Entertainment](https://github.com/orgs/Rimelight-Entertainment/repositories) organization.
