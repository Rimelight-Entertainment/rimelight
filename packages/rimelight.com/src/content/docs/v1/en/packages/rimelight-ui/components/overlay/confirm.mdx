A Confirm component that prompts users for confirmation before completing an action.

## Usage

How to use the Confirm component in its simplest form.

<ComponentShowcase>
  <RLAConfirm triggerLabel="Delete" title="Are you sure?" description="This action cannot be undone." />

  <div slot="code-astro">
    ```astro
    ---
    import RLAConfirm from "@rimelight/ui/components/astro/RLAConfirm.astro";
    ---

    <RLAConfirm triggerLabel="Delete" title="Are you sure?" description="This action cannot be undone." />
    ```

  </div>
</ComponentShowcase>

## Examples

### Destructive Variant

Use the `variant="destructive"` prop to style the confirmation dialog for destructive actions.

<ComponentShowcase>
  <RLAConfirm variant="destructive" triggerLabel="Delete Account" title="Delete Account?" description="This will permanently delete your account." actionLabel="Delete" />

  <div slot="code-astro">
    ```astro
    ---
    import RLAConfirm from "@rimelight/ui/components/astro/RLAConfirm.astro";
    ---

    <RLAConfirm variant="destructive" triggerLabel="Delete Account" title="Delete Account?" description="This will permanently delete your account." actionLabel="Delete" />
    ```

  </div>
</ComponentShowcase>

### Custom Labels

Use `actionLabel` and `cancelLabel` to customize the button labels.

<ComponentShowcase>
  <RLAConfirm triggerLabel="Export Data" title="Export data?" description="Your export will begin shortly." actionLabel="Proceed" cancelLabel="Go Back" />

  <div slot="code-astro">
    ```astro
    ---
    import RLAConfirm from "@rimelight/ui/components/astro/RLAConfirm.astro";
    ---

    <RLAConfirm triggerLabel="Export Data" title="Export data?" description="Your export will begin shortly." actionLabel="Proceed" cancelLabel="Go Back" />
    ```

  </div>
</ComponentShowcase>

### Custom Trigger Slot

You can use the `trigger` slot to provide a custom trigger element instead of `triggerLabel`.

<ComponentShowcase>
  <RLAConfirm title="Confirm Action" description="Please confirm to continue.">
    <button slot="trigger" class="px-4 py-2 bg-neutral text-neutral-foreground rounded hover:bg-neutral/80">
      Open Confirm Dialog
    </button>
  </RLAConfirm>

  <div slot="code-astro">
    ```astro
    ---
    import RLAConfirm from "@rimelight/ui/components/astro/RLAConfirm.astro";
    ---

    <RLAConfirm title="Confirm Action" description="Please confirm to continue.">
      <button slot="trigger" class="px-4 py-2 bg-neutral text-neutral-foreground rounded hover:bg-neutral/80">
        Open Confirm Dialog
      </button>
    </RLAConfirm>
    ```

  </div>
</ComponentShowcase>
