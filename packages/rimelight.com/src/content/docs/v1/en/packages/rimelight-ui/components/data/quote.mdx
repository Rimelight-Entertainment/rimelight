The Quote component displays blockquotes or highlighted statements with a quote icon and caption layout.

## Usage

Provide content for the `quote` and `caption` slots.

<ComponentShowcase>
  <RLAQuote>
    <span slot="quote">Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.</span>
    <span slot="caption">— Leonardo da Vinci</span>
  </RLAQuote>

  <div slot="code-astro">
    ```astro
    ---
    import RLAQuote from "@rimelight/ui/components/astro/RLAQuote.astro";
    ---

    <RLAQuote>
      <span slot="quote">Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.</span>
      <span slot="caption">— Leonardo da Vinci</span>
    </RLAQuote>
    ```

  </div>
</ComponentShowcase>
